# Attribution — bundled real cab IRs

These impulse responses are **community captures** bundled with the
rig-builder plugin so a fresh install has real cab IRs out of the box
(no Rocksmith import, no manual download needed).

They are installed into `<profile>/nam_irs/other/` on first launch
(see `_install_bundled_real_irs` in `routes.py`). Missing files only —
never overwrites a user's own copy.

## Licence

These IRs originate from the **NAM / ToneNET community**, **GGWPTECH
free bass cab IR library**, **Science Amps**, and the **Grebz community
IR collection**. They are redistributed under their respective licences
(typically **CC-BY 4.0**, **CC0**, **free community share**, or
manufacturer free-use terms). Each file's original source and licence
are listed below.

> **Verify before commercial distribution**: community IRs (marked
> "verify") need their upstream licence confirmed. Manufacturer IRs
> (Science Amps) are explicitly free for use with amp sims / IR loaders.
> GGWPTECH and Grebz IRs are freely distributed by their authors for
> non-commercial community use.

## Format

- All files are **48 kHz mono float32 WAV** (the format the engine
  expects). IRs were converted from their original 24-bit PCM /
  16-bit PCM / 32-bit float / 44.1–96 kHz formats and resampled to
  48 kHz, trimmed to ≤ 400 ms (with 10 ms fade-out), and peak-normalised
  to 0.95.
- Loudness calibration (per-cab makeup gain) is computed at runtime by
  `tools/measure_cab_loudness.py` and stored in
  `cab_loudness_makeup.json` — see `configure-feedback-sound.py`
  phase 6 for the standalone re-implementation.

## Clone → real IR mapping

The mapping from synthetic Rocksmith-style cab clones to these real IRs
is defined in `configure-feedback-sound.py` (`DEFAULT_IR_MAP`) and
mirrored in `switch-ir-feedback.py`. All 33 synthetic cab clones are
mapped: 12 guitar, 18 bass, 3 PA/FRFR.
